Year 6 end-of-year unit. 6A and 6B each chose a song (by class vote) and are writing a parody of it for their Farewell; the lyrics come from their literacy class. Alongside, both classes rehearse "Goodbye Footscray City" for the last-day assembly.

Student lyrics and any recordings are student work, so they stay off this site.
